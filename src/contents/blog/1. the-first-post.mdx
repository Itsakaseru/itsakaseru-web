# First!
Welcome to my first blog post! This post marks the release of the blog feature on my website. It’s something I’ve wanted to add for quite a while, well... a long time ago.

The blog is powered by Astro and MDX, which would make it relatively easy for me to add, edit, or remove contents whenever I need to in the future. Commonly used Markdown components are also properly styled now, including headings, lists, tables, code blocks, blockquotes, and a few other things. Hopefully, everything looks consistent and comfortable to read.

Additional components are also added to hopefully help with longer posts, things like Table of Contents should make it easier to navigate through different sections of the blog. There's also a small reading progress card showing how far along or deep you are in the post.

# What I Plan to Share
In this blog, I'll be sharing things that may or may not be related to technology, This could include but not limited to tutorials, my opinions on certain topics, updates about something I’m working on, personal thoughts, and many other things that might hopefully be useful to someone out there.

Even if that someone is just you. Hi! `ヽ(>∀<☆)`

Some posts might be more technical, while others could be more personal or simply me explaining something I found interesting or how I do stuff, etc... I don’t really want to limit this blog to one specific topic, so the content may vary depending on what I feel like writing about at the time.

For things that are more random, chaotic, or possibly contain unchecked or inaccurate information, jokes, memes, and other strange things, I’ll be using a separate [**“Fun”**](/fun) blog section. Think of it as a place for posts that don’t quite fit into for my regular blog.

What kind of things will end up there? Honestly, I don’t know yet. But that’s kind of the point.

# The Future
Of course, the features won’t stop here. If there are other things I’d like to implement in the future, I’ll still continue working for it. The website will keep improving over time even if some changes could take a while to happen.

I’m also considering adding a comment system someday. For now though, I think the blog is good enough as it is. Perhaps if this website gets enough traction and there is enough interest, I’ll add one in the future.

For now, I’d rather focus on writing some posts first. Adding a comment section to an empty blog would be a little awkward anyway `xD`

Welp, that’s it from me. I hope this blog becomes useful to someone out there, or perhaps, at the very least... entertaining to someone.

Thanks for reading! Cya `ヾ( ˃ᴗ˂ )` \*poof\*
