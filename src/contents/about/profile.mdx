# About This Human
---

Hi, you can call me Lemuel, Lemi, Sakii, or Isaka. Most of my friends usually call me Lemi.

I’m just another human being living on Earth. A human who has always been fascinated by technology, whether it is software or hardware. I’m especially curious and dedicated when it comes to programming, and I enjoy exploring how code can be used to solve real-world challenges.

I’m always eager to contribute, learn, and grow in new areas of technology. Currently, I’m working on a big project: making my own game. It is probably going to take a while, but hey, at least I’m trying. As a person, I’d say I’m pretty easy-going. I also consider myself a rather “shy” individual, but I try my best to adapt to different social or professional environments. Sometimes it takes me a bit of time to get comfortable, but once I do, I think I can manage just fine.

I also own two YouTube channels. The first one is [SkyEncripttion](https://youtube.com/SkyEncripttion), which was previously used as a gaming channel. I rarely upload, stream, or even play games nowadays, so maybe it will be used for something else in the future. Although, you can still check it out if you want. It is filled with my old gameplay videos, series, streams, podcasts, and other random things I used to do back then. The second one is [Itsakaseru](https://youtube.com/Itsakaseru), currently I have no idea what I will use it for.

Other than technology, streaming, or gaming, and far away from that world, I’m also interested in all things about dreaming. I got interested in dreams ever since I was a kid. I always wondered how dreams are even possible, why they happen the way they do, and why there are so many unique or strange things happening inside them. Things like lucid dreams also caught my attention, because the idea of being aware inside a dream feels really fascinating to me. I like the feeling of adventuring within my own dreams. Some of the dreams I’ve experienced sparked ideas that made me want to create a story based on what I encountered there. Although, I believe it would be pretty hard, since I rarely write anything, let alone a whole novel. Perhaps if I have enough confidence someday, I’ll try to do it regardless.

For now, I’m still just slowly figuring things out as I go through life, whether it is through programming, creating something, or simply collecting strange ideas from dreams that may or may not become something in the future.

Anyways, that’s it about me in a short introduction. If you want to contact me, you can do it through my email [contact@itsakaseru.me](mailto:contact@itsakaseru.me)
Though I don’t know why you would want to contact me, to be completely honest xD
